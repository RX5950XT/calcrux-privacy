CalcRux 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

一句話

CalcRux 不收集、不傳送、也不分享你的任何個人資料。唯一的網路連線是下載公開匯率，請求裡不帶任何關於你的資訊。

權限與用途

  • 網路（INTERNET）：從 ExchangeRate-API（open.er-api.com）下載公開匯率。只下載，不上傳。
  • 震動（VIBRATE）：按鍵時的觸感回饋。

存在手機上的資料

計算紀錄、設定與匯率快取只存在 App 自己的儲存空間，解除安裝 App 就會一起刪除。如果你開啟了 Android 系統的 Google 備份，這些資料可能會被包含在你自己的裝置備份中；該備份由 Google 處理，適用 Google 隱私權政策，CalcRux 本身無法讀取。

第三方服務

CalcRux 沒有廣告、沒有使用分析，也沒有當機回報服務。下載匯率時，ExchangeRate-API 的伺服器可能依其政策記錄一般連線資訊（例如 IP 位址），這不在 CalcRux 的控制範圍內。

兒童

CalcRux 不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計的，也不會收集任何人的資料。

政策變更

如果政策有變，會更新這一頁並修改上方的生效日期。

聯絡方式

請使用 Google Play 商店頁面上的開發者電子郵件，或到 GitHub Issues 留言。

CalcRux Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short

CalcRux does not collect, transmit or share any of your personal data. Its only network connection downloads public exchange rates, and that request carries no information about you.

Permissions and what they are used for

  • Internet (INTERNET): downloads public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). Download only, nothing is uploaded.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): haptic feedback on key presses.

Data stored on your phone

Calculation history, settings and cached exchange rates are stored only in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you have turned on Android's Google backup, this data may be included in your own device backup, which Google handles under the Google Privacy Policy and which CalcRux itself cannot read.

Third-party services

CalcRux has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. When rates are downloaded, ExchangeRate-API's servers may log standard connection information (such as IP address) under their own policy, which is outside CalcRux's control.

Children

CalcRux is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date above.
